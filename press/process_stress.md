# Process: Stress

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Initiated by
A demand exceeds available resources.
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) is perceived, not measured.
The [Signal](piece_signal.md) fires in the body before the mind names it.

## Direction
Toward resolution of the [Gap](piece_gap.md).
Not toward comfort.
The system moves to close what it cannot tolerate staying open.

## Lever
The carrying force: survival circuitry, faster than thought, already routing.
The resistance: the cost of each available route -- [Fight](process_fight.md), [Flight](process_flight.md), [Freeze](process_freeze.md), [Fawn](process_fawn.md) -- is not zero.
The process holds both. It does not resolve them. It routes through one.

## Echo
The chosen route inherits the full activation of the Gap.
The route taken leaves a trace.
The next Gap finds the same route cheaper.
What was a response becomes a [Pattern](piece_pattern.md).
