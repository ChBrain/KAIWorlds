# Press [↓](https://github.com/ChBrain/KAIWorlds/releases/latest/download/press.zip)

*Stress is not what happens to you. It is what runs through you before you decide.*

A KAI World built on the stress response. One family. Five members. One house. The press runs between them before anyone speaks.

---

## What It Is

Press is a world for exploring how stress moves through people and between them. It is not a therapy tool. It is not a stress management system. It is a world where the mechanisms of stress are embodied in five fictional characters who do not know they are running them.

The agent enters as whoever is carrying the most active press. It shifts when the press moves.
