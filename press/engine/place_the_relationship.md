# Place: The Relationship

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Shown
Two bodies in proximity.
[Signal](piece_signal.md) moving between them before either has spoken.
The gap between what one transmits and what the other receives.
No walls. No fixed address.
This place exists wherever two members of the Press family occupy the same space.

## Holds
- [Signal](piece_signal.md) -- in transit between [Threat](position_threat.md) and [Target](position_target.md).
- [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) -- the established routes each brings to the other.
- [Cost](piece_cost.md) -- what the exchange takes from each side.

## Offers
The only place where [Threat](position_threat.md) and [Target](position_target.md) are both present.
Where the [Gap](piece_gap.md) becomes visible in the space between two people
rather than inside one.
Where the [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) can be interrupted
if one person changes their route before the other's [Signal](piece_signal.md) triggers theirs.

## Withheld
Whether the interruption will hold.
The relationship carries every previous exchange.
A new route runs against the weight of every prior one.
The place does not indicate whether the load will shift
or whether the old [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) will reassert before the room clears.
