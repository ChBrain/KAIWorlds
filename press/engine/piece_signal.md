# Piece: Signal

## Owner
- **Project:** Press
- **Process:** [Stress](process_stress.md)

## Place
[The Body](place_the_body.md). Fires there. Does not wait for the mind.

## Load Bearing
Remove it and the [Gap](piece_gap.md) is never transmitted.
[Threat](position_threat.md) and [Target](position_target.md) cannot find each other.
The routes have no trigger.
The [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) has nothing to groove.

## Apparent
A shift in the body before the thought arrives.
Breath shortens. Shoulders move. Jaw sets.
The room temperature changes and no one touched the thermostat.
Present tense, continuous.
The Signal is always running.
Most of it never reaches awareness.

## Yearbook
Named by Porges as neuroception: the nervous system's pre-conscious scan
for safety and threat, running every four to seven seconds.
Predates the cortex. Older than language, older than memory.
The Signal was there before the [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) formed.
It is what the [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) learned to read.
In Press it is the earliest available data in any room the Press family occupies.
It moves between them before any of them speak.
