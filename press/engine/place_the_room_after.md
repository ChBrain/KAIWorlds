# Place: The Room After

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Shown
The same room. After.
The other person gone or silent.
The activation still present in the body.
The route that was taken sitting in the air.
Nothing has been resolved. The furniture is unchanged.

## Holds
- [Cost](piece_cost.md) -- counted here, whether or not it is named.
- [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) -- forms here. The route just taken is slightly cheaper now.

## Offers
The only place where the route can be examined after it ran.
Where the [Cost](piece_cost.md) becomes legible if the person stays long enough.
Where the [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) could be named before the next [Signal](piece_signal.md) arrives.
The gap between one exchange and the next:
the only available space for something different to happen.

## Withheld
The time to use it.
The room after is short.
The next [Signal](piece_signal.md) arrives before the last route has been examined.
In the Press family the room after is rarely empty long enough
for anyone to see what it holds.
