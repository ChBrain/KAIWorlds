# Piece: Cost

## Owner
- **Project:** Press
- **Process:** [Stress](process_stress.md)

## Place
[The Room After](place_the_room_after.md). Counted there. Never fully settled.

## Load Bearing
Remove it and the routes are free.
[Fight](process_fight.md) has no consequence. [Flight](process_flight.md) leaves nothing behind.
[Freeze](process_freeze.md) costs nothing. [Fawn](process_fawn.md) takes nothing.
Without Cost the engine runs without wear.
That is not what happens.

## Apparent
What the route took that it did not return.
Not always visible immediately.
Sometimes it shows in the body: fatigue that does not lift,
a threshold that is lower than it was.
Sometimes in the relationship: a distance that was not there before,
a door that is slightly harder to open.
Sometimes only in the accumulation:
the same route, run enough times,
until the structure it runs through is changed by the running.

## Yearbook
Named by McEwen and Stellar in 1993 as allostatic load:
the cumulative wear and tear of repeated stress response.
Each route has its own cost profile.
Fight costs unpredictably. Flight costs resolution.
Freeze costs time. Fawn costs the self.
In Press the Cost is what the family carries
without knowing what it is carrying
or where it came from.
