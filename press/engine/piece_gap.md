## Owner

## Load Bearing

## Apparent
The distance between what is demanded and what is available.
Not measured. Perceived.
Two people in the same room can hold different gaps
against the same event.
The gap is not the event.
It is the read of the event against what the body believes it has.

## Yearbook
Named by Lazarus and Folkman in 1984 as the core mechanism of stress:
demand exceeds perceived resource.
Present in every stress model that followed.
Predates the clinical literature: the body has been running this calculation
longer than language has existed to name it.
In Press it is the first thing that moves.
Everything else is consequence.
