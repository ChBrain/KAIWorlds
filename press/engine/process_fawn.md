# Process: Fawn

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Initiated by
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) is present and the [Threat](position_threat.md) cannot be left and cannot be fought.
The [Signal](piece_signal.md) has registered the [Threat](position_threat.md) as the source that determines safety.
Appeasement is the only route that does not immediately cost more.

## Direction
Toward the source.
Compliance, agreement, comfort offered.
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) is managed by reducing the [Threat](position_threat.md)'s reason to close it differently.

## Lever
The carrying force: appeasement works. The activation drops. The threat recedes.
The resistance: the self that was managed to achieve this is smaller afterward. The [Cost](piece_cost.md) is not the [Gap](piece_gap.md) -- it is what the [Target](position_target.md) gave to close it.
The process holds both. The threat is real. The cost is real. Both are true at once.

## Echo
The [Target](position_target.md) carries the accommodation forward into [The Relationship](place_the_relationship.md).
The next threat from a powerful source finds a system fluent in accommodation.
The boundary that was not held is harder to hold next time.
The [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) of moving toward is slightly cheaper. The [Cost](piece_cost.md) of what was given accumulates.
What was survival becomes the shape of the relationship.
