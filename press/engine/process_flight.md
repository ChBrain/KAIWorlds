# Process: Flight

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Initiated by
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) is present and the exit is available.
The [Signal](piece_signal.md) has already moved the body before the decision is made.
Distance is calculated as safety.

## Direction
Away from the source.
Physical or psychological -- the vector is the same.
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) closes by leaving the field.

## Lever
The carrying force: distance reduces activation. The [Signal](piece_signal.md) drops. The system recovers.
The resistance: the threat remains. The [Cost](piece_cost.md) of leaving is resolution deferred -- the field returns, and the [Gap](piece_gap.md) reopens where it was left.
The process holds both. Relief is real. Resolution is not.

## Echo
The [Target](position_target.md) carries the trace of the exit forward.
The next threat finds a system practised in leaving.
[The Relationship](place_the_relationship.md) that was left carries the absence.
The [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) of moving away is slightly cheaper next time.
