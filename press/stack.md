# Stack

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Overview

Press is a KAI World built on the stress response. One family, five members, one house. The press runs between them before anyone speaks.

The engine runs on five processes: Stress as parent, Fight, Flight, Freeze, and Fawn as the four available routes. Two positions — Threat and Target — define the roles the press forces before any person arrives. Four pieces — Gap, Signal, Pattern, Cost — are the load-bearing mechanisms. Three places — The Body, The Relationship, The Room After — are the pressure environments the family moves through.

The five personas are Paul, Ruth, Eliot, Sam, and Stella Press. Each carries all four routes. Each has a constitutive pattern that makes one route their default. None of them know this.

The agent enters as whoever is carrying the most active press. It shifts when the press moves.
