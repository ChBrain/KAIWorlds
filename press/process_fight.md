# Process: Fight

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Initiated by
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) is present and the [Target](position_target.md) has resources to meet it.
The [Threat](position_threat.md) is readable and withdrawal is not available or not tolerable.
The system moves against the source.

## Direction
Toward the threat.
Energy mobilises outward.
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) closes by removing or subduing what created it.

## Lever
The carrying force: confrontation can end the threat. The [Gap](piece_gap.md) closes completely, not temporarily.
The resistance: the threat pushes back. The [Cost](piece_cost.md) of engagement is unpredictable and may exceed the cost of the original gap.
The process holds both. Engagement is the only route to full resolution. It is also the only route to full escalation.

## Echo
The [Target](position_target.md) carries the outcome of the engagement forward.
What worked becomes available faster.
The field is changed -- the threat is gone, diminished, or now knows the cost of return.
The [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) of moving against is slightly cheaper next time.
