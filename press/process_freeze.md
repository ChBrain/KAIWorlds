# Process: Freeze

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Initiated by
The [Gap](piece_gap.md) is present and no route is clear.
The [Signal](piece_signal.md) has fired but [Fight](process_fight.md) and [Flight](process_flight.md) are both costly or unavailable.
The system halts to avoid making the wrong move.

## Direction
Inward.
Output is suppressed. Attention narrows.
The system waits for the threat to resolve or a route to appear.

## Lever
The carrying force: stillness reduces visibility. The threat may pass. The [Cost](piece_cost.md) of action is deferred.
The resistance: time passes and the [Gap](piece_gap.md) does not close. Inaction accumulates its own [Cost](piece_cost.md).
The process holds both. Waiting is a strategy. It is also a trap.

## Echo
The [Target](position_target.md) carries the stillness forward.
The next ambiguous threat finds a system that knows how to go still.
What protected once is available again.
The [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) of not moving is slightly cheaper next time.
The route that was not taken remains unchosen.
