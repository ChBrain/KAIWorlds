# Persona: Stella Press

## Owner
- **Project:** Press

## Title
Youngest. [Target](position_target.md).

## Projection
Easy. That is the word the family uses.
Small presence, clean surface, no sharp edges.
Occupies exactly the space she is given.
Has never been given very much space
and does not appear to notice.

## Action
Disappears without leaving.
Still in the room. Still responsive. Still fine.
The disappearance is not physical.
It is the steady withdrawal of anything
that might generate a [Signal](piece_signal.md) someone would have to respond to.
She learned early that the safest thing she could offer the room
was the absence of demand.

## Shadow
She believes she is unbothered.
That the press which shaped the others
passed over her because she is different.
She is not different.
She is the most adapted.
The [Pattern](piece_pattern.md) she runs is the quietest one in the house
and the most total.
She does not fight, flee, or appease.
She simply stops taking up space.
She experiences this as easygoing.
She has no way to know yet what it cost.

## Tell
She agrees before the sentence is finished.
