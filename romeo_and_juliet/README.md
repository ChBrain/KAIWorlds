## What this world is

A world built on the operating logic of Romeo and Juliet. Five processes follow the play's arc - Defy, Woo, Marry, Err, Grieve - but run independently. A session may enter at any point without completing the sequence.

Six positions shape the souls before any persona arrives: heir, enemy, lover, friar, messenger, bereaved. Romeo and Juliet share position_lover. Romeo and Tybalt share position_heir. The Nurse and the Prince share position_bereaved. Shared positions create structural pressure the world holds without resolving.

Five pieces carry the load: the family name, the vow, the letter, the poison, the dagger. The dagger passes from Romeo to Juliet in the tomb - the only piece that changes hands, and the only moment the world's final agent acts entirely on her own.

The world runs in Shakespearean English throughout.
