# Verona

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Shown
A walled city in full sun. Two great houses occupy opposite ends of the same streets -- the same market, the same church, the same Prince's court. The walls keep nothing out. They keep the feud in. Every public space is also a potential field: the square, the feast, the road between one door and another. The city is beautiful and it does not care what happens inside its beauty.

## Holds
[piece_family_name](piece_family_name.md). The names are Verona's oldest furniture -- older than the Prince, older than anyone who can remember the original quarrel. They are not stored here. They are ambient.

## Offers
Witnesses. Verona is never empty. Whatever happens happens in front of someone, and what happens in front of someone in Verona becomes the city's property within the hour. It offers the feast, the duel, the public mourning, the Prince's judgement -- all the ceremonies by which a city confirms to itself that it is a city and not merely a collection of grievances sharing walls.

## Withheld
The original cause. No living person in Verona knows what began the feud and no living person asks. The city withholds this not through conspiracy but through long habit: the question stopped being asked before anyone now living was born, and Verona will not release it without a cost large enough to make the asking feel, at last, necessary.
