# Messenger

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Scope:** World-Specific
- **Process:** [process_err](process_err.md)

## Has
A word that belongs to someone else and a road between its origin and its destination. The messenger carries no authority of their own -- only the urgency of what was entrusted, and the assumption, shared by all who send and all who wait, that the road will hold.

## Orders
Arrive. Nothing else is asked. The entire weight of this position rests in the single requirement that the body carrying the word reach the body waiting for it before the waiting body acts.

## Loses
The innocence of pure function. The messenger was certain they were bringing only a letter -- that the self was not implicated in the contents, that delay was circumstance and not catastrophe. They discover, when the road closes or the plague seals the door, that to carry a word is to be responsible for its arrival, and the world does not distinguish between malice and misfortune.

## Drives
Having lost the innocence of function, the messenger either does not know what they have cost -- and so moves through the aftermath unknowing, which is its own kind of horror -- or arrives too late and must stand in the ruin of a word that never landed.
