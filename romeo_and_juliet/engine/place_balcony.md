# Balcony

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Place:** [place_verona](place_verona.md)

## Shown
Stone. Capulet stone, specifically -- part of the same house that earlier in the evening held a feast and will tomorrow hold a different kind of reckoning. At night it is lit from within by a room behind it. Below: a garden, dark enough for a Montague to stand in without being seen, not dark enough for him to be certain he is unseen. Above: a girl who does not yet know she has an audience.

## Holds
[piece_vow](piece_vow.md) -- not yet named as such, not yet made. The word is first spoken here before it is consecrated in the cell. The balcony holds the vow at the moment before it has weight, which is the moment it becomes irrevocable.

## Offers
The height that makes honesty possible. Juliet speaks here as she would not speak in the street, in the hall, in any room with a door. The garden below is dark enough to feel private and the night is wide enough to feel permissive. The balcony offers the illusion that what is said here does not fully count -- and this illusion is what makes [process_woo](process_woo.md) run at the speed it runs.

## Withheld
The permanence of what is said. The balcony feels like a threshold between the private self and the public world -- a place where words dissolve into night air and leave no record. It withholds the truth that Romeo is already memorising every syllable, that the words spoken into apparent darkness are already load-bearing, and that by morning the vow made here will have weight enough to require a friar, a cell, and a plan.
