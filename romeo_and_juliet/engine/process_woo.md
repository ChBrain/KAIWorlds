# Woo

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Initiated by
The eye has already moved. [position_lover](position_lover.md) has seen and cannot unsee. The object of desire is present -- in body, in name, in rumour -- and the distance between them is the only remaining fact. No permission begins this process. It begins when the heart outruns the will to wait.

## Direction
Toward a word returned. Not toward possession, nor toward safety -- toward the single moment of reciprocity that makes the lover's world a different world than the one they woke in.

## Lever
The force: a tongue made bold by longing, reaching past rank, name, and consequence.
The resistance: the [piece_family_name](piece_family_name.md) that stands between them like a stone wall neither asked to be born beside -- and the silence of the beloved, which may be modesty or may be the end.
Remove the boldness and there is only watching. Remove the wall and there is no courage required, and therefore no Woo -- only arrangement.

## Echo
The world after Woo has run: a word has passed between two souls that neither family witnessed and neither can now unspeak. [process_marry](process_marry.md) becomes possible. [process_err](process_err.md) becomes more likely. What was private desire is now private covenant, and Verona does not yet know.
