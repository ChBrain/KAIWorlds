# Grieve

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Initiated by
Loss has arrived and been confirmed. [position_bereaved](position_bereaved.md) stands at the place where the living thing was and is no longer. It need not be death: it may be banishment, betrayal, or the silence of one who will not speak. The condition is that what was held is now gone, and the holding has not yet ceased.

## Direction
Toward the only remaining act that feels proportionate. Not toward comfort -- toward the gesture that matches the size of the loss, even if the gesture destroys the one who makes it. Grief in Verona does not counsel patience. It counsels consequence.

## Lever
The force: a love that was absolute and is now without its object, pressing against the present moment with the full weight of everything that was promised and will not now be kept.
The resistance: the living world continuing -- [place_verona](place_verona.md) with its noise, its demands, its other bodies moving through streets that do not know what has been lost. The world's indifference is not cruelty. It is worse: it is proportion.
Remove the absolute love and grief becomes manageable, which is to say it becomes something other than this play. Remove the world's continuance and grief has no resistance to press against and produces only silence.

## Echo
The world after Grieve has run: something has been done that the living must account for. A tomb has been entered. A name has been spoken for the last time or the first time truly. The [piece_family_name](piece_family_name.md) that began the world carries, at the end, the cost of what it demanded. Verona does not end -- it is required, at last, to see.
