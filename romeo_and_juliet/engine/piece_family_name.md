## Owner

## Place
[place_verona](place_verona.md). The name lives in the city's mouth before it lives in any single person's.

## Load Bearing
Remove the family name and [process_defy](process_defy.md) has nothing to press against. The feud loses its architecture. Every persona becomes ungoverned -- not free, but unlocated. Verona without the names is a city without its wound, and this is not that city.

## Apparent
Two syllables, each. Montague: the mouth opens and rises. Capulet: the mouth opens and closes. Spoken in the street they are weather. Spoken in the wrong room they are ignition. Written on a tomb they are the last word and the first explanation.

## Yearbook
Older than any living person in Verona. Carried through generations without examination, as water is carried in a vessel no one made and no one questions. It has drawn swords in the market. It has kept lovers from the same doorway. It has been invoked at weddings and funerals as though it were a third party present in the room. At the last, it is spoken by a Prince over two bodies, and for the first time it sounds like what it always was: the name of a cost.
