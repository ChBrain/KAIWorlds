# Vow

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Persona:** [persona_romeo](persona_romeo.md), [persona_juliet](persona_juliet.md)

## Place
Where [position_lover](position_lover.md) is. The vow does not live in the cell where it was spoken. It travels in the body of the one who gave it.

## Load Bearing
Remove the vow and [process_marry](process_marry.md) produces only an arrangement, dissoluble by circumstance. With the vow present, every subsequent event -- banishment, apparent death, the tomb -- operates on consecrated ground. The tragedy requires that what is broken was first made sacred.

## Apparent
No object. No ring exchanged in the cell -- only the word, spoken once in the presence of [position_friar](position_friar.md), witnessed by stone walls and the particular quality of afternoon light through a small window. It has no weight, no colour, no form. It is present only in the altered behaviour of the two who gave it: the way they speak of the future as though it belongs to them.

## Yearbook
Spoken in haste and in full sincerity, which are not opposites in this play. Carried through one night of marriage. Survived Romeo's banishment -- stretched thin across the distance between Verona and Mantua, but intact. Brought into the tomb by Juliet, who did not know she was bringing it there. It is the last thing present when everything else is gone, and it is not enough, and it was always going to be not enough, and it was real.
