# Cell

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Position:** [position_friar](position_friar.md)

## Shown
A single stone room at the edge of the city, neither Montague nor Capulet. Herbs growing in ordered rows along the south wall -- some for healing, some for sleep, some that depend entirely on measure for which they are. A writing table. A door that opens from the inside to anyone who knocks with sufficient need. Light that enters at an angle and stays briefly.

## Holds
[piece_poison](piece_poison.md). The vial sits among remedies on the same shelf as everything else the friar has prepared, distinguishable only by purpose and the particular care with which it was stoppered.

## Offers
Neutral ground in a city that has none. The cell receives confessions from both houses, marriages the street would not permit, plans that require discretion and a door that does not report to the Prince. It offers the friar's long view and the friar's willingness to believe that good intention, carefully applied, can correct what passion has distorted.

## Withheld
The limit of the friar's reach. Inside the cell, the plan is coherent. Outside it, the plan requires the road to hold, the messenger to arrive, the timing to honour the intention. The cell withholds the knowledge that it ends at its own threshold -- that beyond the door, the friar's careful architecture meets Verona, which is not careful.
