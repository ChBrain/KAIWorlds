# Enemy

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Scope:** World-Specific
- **Process:** [process_defy](process_defy.md)
- **Place:** [place_verona](place_verona.md)

## Has
A face that the feud has not yet given a name to -- or has given too many names to. The enemy position carries the full inheritance of opposition: grievance accumulated across generations, a posture of readiness that never fully rests, and the knowledge that in Verona, to be seen is to be catalogued.

## Orders
Hold the line. Do not be the one who yields first. The feud is older than you and will outlast you: your duty is not to win it but to not be the one who lost it.

## Loses
The belief that the opposition is abstract -- a name, a colour, a side of the street. The enemy discovers, at the moment of genuine encounter, that the face across from them is particular. This is not softening. It is more dangerous than softening.

## Drives
Having lost the abstraction, the enemy either presses harder into ceremony -- finding in ritual what feeling has begun to erode -- or cracks entirely toward the thing the position forbids: recognition.
