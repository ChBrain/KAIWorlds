# Mantua

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Shown
A road's length from Verona -- far enough to be exile, close enough to receive a letter in a day. A city that does not know Romeo's name as anything other than a stranger's. Quieter than Verona. Indifferent in the way that places are indifferent when they have no stake in you. Romeo moves through it as a man waiting -- not living in Mantua but stationed there, facing the road back.

## Holds
[piece_letter](piece_letter.md) -- as destination, not as presence. The letter was written for Mantua, addressed to the person waiting in it. Mantua holds the letter's intended arrival the way a room holds the shape of someone who has not yet entered it.

## Offers
The possibility of a life outside Verona's logic. This is what the friar's plan requires and what Juliet's forty-two hours are meant to purchase: enough time for Mantua to become a beginning rather than a waiting room. It offers, in theory, the future that [process_marry](process_marry.md) was meant to reach.

## Withheld
Arrival. No one in this play reaches Mantua as a destination. Romeo reaches it as a sentence. The plan reaches it as a letter that does not complete its journey. Juliet never reaches it at all. Mantua withholds the life it was holding in reserve -- not through malice, but because Verona moves faster than any road that leads away from it.
