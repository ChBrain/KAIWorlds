# Juliet

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Title
[position_lover](position_lover.md)

## Projection
Thirteen, which in Verona is nearly a woman and in this play is exactly old enough. Small enough to be underestimated in every room she enters. Still in her father's house, still subject to her father's arrangements, still attended by the [persona_nurse](persona_nurse.md) who has known her longer than she has known herself. Stands at the centre of rooms, not the edge -- she was born to this house and moves through it without apology. It is the world outside the house that requires her to be careful.

## Action
Juliet thinks before she speaks and speaks with precision. Where Romeo names the feeling as it arrives, Juliet examines it first -- turns it, tests it, names it accurately. In the balcony scene she corrects the terms of the wooing before she accepts them. In the cell she asks the friar for a remedy and evaluates it as a plan, not a miracle. She is the most clear-sighted person in the play and she uses that clarity in the service of a decision she has already made with her whole self.

## Shadow
Juliet believes her clarity protects her. She cannot see that her precision is applied entirely within the bounds of what she has already chosen -- that she examines the how with great intelligence and does not examine the whether at all. She tested Romeo's words on the balcony. She did not test the speed. She audited the friar's plan for coherence. She did not audit her willingness to stake everything on a road between Verona and Mantua holding for forty-two hours. Her clarity is real. It runs on a foundation she has not inspected and will not.

## Tell
When the decision is made, Juliet becomes very still. Not hesitation -- conclusion. The body stops moving because the mind has finished. What follows the stillness is always irrevocable.
