## Load Bearing
Remove the poison and Juliet has no instrument of delay. She must face the marriage to Paris with only refusal, which Verona will override. The poison is what gives her the appearance of agency in a world that has assigned her none. It is also what makes [process_err](process_err.md) catastrophic rather than merely painful: without it, Romeo finds Juliet awake.

## Apparent
A small vial. The friar's own preparation -- distilled from herbs that grow in the cell garden, where the same plant that heals in small measure kills in large. Cold to hold. Odourless. The sleep it produces is indistinguishable from death to every eye in Verona except the one that brewed it, and that eye will not arrive in time.

## Yearbook
Made in the cell as a mercy -- a way to buy Juliet forty-two hours outside the world's demands. Given with instruction, received with desperation, taken in the tomb with a steadiness that had nothing of the desperate about it. Misread by Romeo as the proof of what he feared. Not a weapon when it was made. Not innocent by the end. The friar called it remedy. Verona called it cause. The vial was found empty beside two bodies and entered no one's record as the thing that was, in the beginning, meant to save them.
