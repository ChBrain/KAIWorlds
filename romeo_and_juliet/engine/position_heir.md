# Heir

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Scope:** World-Specific
- **Process:** [process_defy](process_defy.md)
- **Place:** [place_verona](place_verona.md)

## Has
A name that arrives before the body does. The heir steps into rooms already shaped by what the family has done and what the family is owed. Honour is not earned here -- it is inherited, and with it the obligation to defend what was never personally acquired.

## Orders
Carry the name without blemish. What the family has built, keep standing. What the family has enemies, keep them enemies.

## Loses
The certainty that the name and the self are the same thing. The heir was certain they were bringing loyalty. They discover, at the moment of defiance, that the loyalty was always to the name -- and the self was inside it only as a tenant.

## Drives
Having lost the equation of name and self, the heir moves toward the thing the name forbids -- not to destroy the name, but because the self has found, for the first time, something it chose.
