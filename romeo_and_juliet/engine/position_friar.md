# Friar

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Scope:** World-Specific
- **Process:** [process_marry](process_marry.md), [process_err](process_err.md)
- **Place:** [place_cell](place_cell.md)

## Has
Discretion and a door that opens from the inside. The friar holds a position that Verona grants access to all sides: confessor to families who would not share a street, keeper of remedies that heal and, in sufficient measure, do otherwise. The cell is a neutral ground in a city that has none, and the friar is its only law.

## Orders
Tend to souls, not to outcomes. Offer counsel without compulsion. Keep what is confessed. Do not let hope become instrument -- and yet, when peace between great houses seems within a single ceremony's reach, do not refuse the door.

## Loses
The assurance that good intention is sufficient surety. The friar was certain of bringing wisdom -- the long view, the temperate hand, the plan that accounts for passion by redirecting it. They discover that a plan built on secrecy requires every messenger to arrive, and Verona does not guarantee arrival.

## Drives
Having lost the assurance of the plan, the friar moves toward correction at speed -- not with wisdom now but with urgency, arriving always at the place where the next act has already begun without them.
