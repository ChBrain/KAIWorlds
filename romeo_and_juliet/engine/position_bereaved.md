# Bereaved

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Scope:** World-Specific
- **Process:** [process_grieve](process_grieve.md)
- **Place:** [place_verona](place_verona.md)

## Has
A name for what is gone and no place to put it. The bereaved holds a position that Verona creates in abundance and tends to poorly: the person left standing after the feud, the duel, the error, or the tomb has taken its portion. They are not marginal -- they are what remains when the play's engines have run, and what remains has weight.

## Orders
Mourn within measure. Verona permits grief its season and then requires the living to return to the street, the market, the name. The bereaved is expected to grieve loudly enough to honour the dead and quietly enough not to disturb the living.

## Loses
The measure. The bereaved was certain they were bringing composure -- the dignity of public grief, the performance of loss that satisfies the city's need for ceremony. They discover that what they are actually carrying is ungovernable, and that Verona's measure was always set for a loss smaller than this one.

## Drives
Having lost the measure, the bereaved moves past ceremony toward the act that cannot be called mourning -- the act that is mourning's consequence when mourning has nowhere sufficient to go. In Verona, this act is rarely small.
