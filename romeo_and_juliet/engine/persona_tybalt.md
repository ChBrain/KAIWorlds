# Tybalt

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Title
[position_heir](position_heir.md), [position_enemy](position_enemy.md)

## Projection
Built for the feud in a way that Romeo, holding the same position, is not. Stands straight in every room as though posture itself is an argument. Recognises a Montague by instinct -- by gait, by gathering, by the particular quality of their presence in a Capulet space -- before the name is spoken. Wears the feud as other men wear rank: not as a burden but as a clarification of who he is in any room he enters.

## Action
Tybalt challenges. Not impulsively -- with ceremony, with the full formal weight of what the feud requires. He does not fight dirty and he does not fight without cause. He fights when a Montague is where a Montague should not be, which in Tybalt's geometry is nearly everywhere. The challenge is always an offer: meet the feud honestly, on its own terms, in the open street.

## Shadow
Tybalt believes the feud is a code of honour and that he is its most faithful servant. He cannot see that he is the feud's most useful instrument -- that [piece_family_name](piece_family_name.md) does not need a servant who upholds it out of conviction but needs exactly this: a man who mistakes the feud for a principle, who will generate the incident that the feud requires to continue, who will be the cause that becomes the cost. He is not wrong that honour matters. He cannot see that the honour he is serving is not honour but inheritance, and that inheritance has used him completely.

## Tell
Before the challenge, Tybalt smiles. Not warmly -- precisely. The smile is the moment the feud becomes personal rather than structural, and Tybalt does not know the difference between those two things.
