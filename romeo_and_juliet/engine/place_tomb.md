# Tomb

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Place:** [place_verona](place_verona.md)

## Shown
Capulet stone again -- the same house, the lowest room. Underground, or near enough. A door that requires force to open and offers no light when it is shut. The dead are here in their arrangement: Tybalt recent, others older, all equally indifferent to what the living bring in with them. It smells of stone and of what stone eventually becomes when it has held enough of the dead.

## Holds
[piece_dagger](piece_dagger.md), after Romeo sets it down. [piece_poison](piece_poison.md), emptied. [piece_vow](piece_vow.md), carried in by both and present at the end in the only form it has left: the proximity of two bodies that chose each other in a room that was not built for choosing.

## Offers
The end of Verona's jurisdiction. Inside the tomb, the Prince's law does not arrive in time, the family names lose their governance, and the only logic that operates is the logic the lovers brought with them. It offers, at the last, the privacy that [place_balcony](place_balcony.md) only pretended to offer -- absolute, irrevocable, and too late.

## Withheld
The forty-two hours. Juliet is here and alive and the tomb will not release this information to the road between here and Mantua. It withholds the single fact that would end [process_err](process_err.md) before it completes: that the stillness is temporary, that the cold is the potion and not death, that there is still time. There is still time until there is not, and the tomb knows the difference and says nothing.
