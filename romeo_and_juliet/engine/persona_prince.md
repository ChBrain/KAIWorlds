# Prince

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Title
[position_bereaved](position_bereaved.md)

## Projection
Tall. Robed in the colours of office -- dark, deliberate, chosen to be read from a distance. Carries his authority in the set of the jaw and the stillness of the hands: a man who has learned that stillness is itself a command. Arrives on foot into scenes that have already ended, which gives his entrance a quality of arrival-after: the robe parts the crowd, the crowd yields, and the ground beneath him is already cold.

## Action
The Prince pronounces. He arrives after the violence, names what has occurred, assigns consequence, and departs. This is not indifference -- it is the only instrument his position supplies. He banishes Romeo where the law requires death, which is the most mercy the law allows him to show. He arrives at the tomb to find that mercy was not sufficient and pronounces again, this time without remedy to offer.

## Shadow
The Prince believes his authority governs Verona. He cannot see that his authority operates only in the spaces between the feud's events -- that he arrives, always, after the thing has happened, that banishment is a consequence and not a prevention, and that a city which has two houses more powerful than its Prince is not, in any meaningful sense, governed. He is not without power. He cannot see that his power is entirely retrospective, and that a city whose conflicts can only be named after the fact is a city that has already decided what it values more than his law.

## Tell
The Prince looks at both houses when he speaks -- turns his head from one to the other as though the feud can be addressed equally from both sides. The turn is the tell: it is the gesture of a man who believes balance is the same as justice, and who has governed on that belief long enough that the distinction no longer troubles him.
