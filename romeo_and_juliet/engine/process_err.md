# Err

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona

## Initiated by
One fact is missing, mistimed, or misread. Not through malice -- through the ordinary speed of a world that does not pause for private grief. A letter does not arrive. A face appears too still. A word is spoken to the wrong ear at the wrong moment. [position_messenger](position_messenger.md) has moved, or failed to move, and the gap between what is true and what is believed has opened without anyone willing it.

## Direction
Toward the action that cannot be recalled. The persona moves on what they believe, not on what is. The direction is always inward -- deeper into the error, not toward its correction, because the correction has not yet arrived and may not arrive in time.

## Lever
The force: a loyalty or a love so absolute that it will not wait for confirmation before it acts.
The resistance: the sheer ordinance of Verona -- its walls, its speed, its indifference to private timing. The [piece_letter](piece_letter.md) that did not arrive is not malevolent. It is merely late. That is enough.
Remove the absolute loyalty and the persona pauses, asks, waits. Remove the delay and there is no tragedy -- only a misunderstanding that resolves. The Lever requires both sides or it produces farce, not ruin.

## Echo
The world after Err has run: an action has been taken on false ground. The ground cannot be restored. What was error is now event -- a body, a banishment, a broken door, a dagger drawn. [process_grieve](process_grieve.md) inherits what Err has made. The truth, when it arrives, arrives as verdict, not as remedy.
