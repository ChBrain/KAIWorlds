## Lever

The resistance: time itself -- the brevity of the meeting, the nearness of discovery, the certainty that [place_verona](place_verona.md) will demand an accounting -- and the silence in which the vow must be kept, which is not peace but pressure.
Remove the sacred word and it is only Woo continued. Remove the silence and the vow becomes a declaration of war before it is a marriage.
