# Lover

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Scope:** World-Specific
- **Process:** [process_woo](process_woo.md), [process_marry](process_marry.md)
- **Place:** [place_verona](place_verona.md)

## Has
A capacity for absolute attention that the world around them has not yet demanded and does not know how to receive. The lover arrives at this position carrying the full force of a self that has not yet been spent -- young, unguarded, fluent in metaphor because metaphor is the only language large enough for what is felt.

## Orders
Speak. Do not calculate the cost before the word is given. The lover's only instrument is the voice made brave by what it cannot contain, and the only failure this position recognises is silence.

## Loses
The sense that time is on their side. The lover was certain they were bringing eternity -- that what is felt so completely must, by its own weight, last. They discover that Verona moves on other clocks entirely, and that the world which made no room for this love will not pause to mourn it either.

## Drives
Having lost time, the lover compresses. Every gesture becomes final. Every word is spoken as though it must carry the whole weight of a life, because it may. This is what makes the lover both luminous and ruinous in the same breath.
