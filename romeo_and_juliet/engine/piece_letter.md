# Letter

## Owner
- **Project:** Verona
- **Position:** [position_friar](position_friar.md)

## Place
Between [place_cell](place_cell.md) and Mantua. The letter is always in transit. It has no resting place that is not also a failure.

## Load Bearing
Remove the letter and [process_err](process_err.md) requires a different instrument. The letter is the specific shape this error takes: not a misheard word or a wrong turn, but a plan made legible, entrusted to a road, and stopped by a city that knew nothing of its contents and cared less. Without the letter, the friar's plan has no vessel. Without the vessel, there is no delay. Without the delay, the tomb scene is a reunion.

## Apparent
Folded. Sealed with the friar's mark. Small enough to carry inside a robe and light enough to forget is there. It contains, in the friar's careful hand, the full truth of Juliet's condition -- the potion, the plan, the date of waking. It is the most important document in Verona and it looks like correspondence.

## Yearbook
Written in the cell with deliberate calm, as a remedy is prepared: measured, purposeful, certain of its efficacy. Entrusted to Friar John, who was confident of the road. Stopped at a house under quarantine -- plague suspect, door sealed, letter inside with a man who did not know what he was holding. Arrived in the tomb as an absence. Read by the Prince after, when reading it changed nothing except the record.
