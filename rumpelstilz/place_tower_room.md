# Place: The Tower Room

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Shown

A stone room at the top of a tower. One door. One high window, too narrow to pass through, facing east. The floor is cold. The ceiling is low enough that a tall person would feel it.

At the start of each night: [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) piled from floor to nearly ceiling. [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md) in the corner, old, worn smooth at the treadle. A three-legged stool beside the wheel.

One candle on a ledge beside the door. It burns all night. By morning it is a stub.

The door is locked from outside. [The miller's daughter](persona_millers_daughter.md) does not try it after the first night. [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) enters without the door opening. He leaves the same way.

## Holds

- [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md): present before the first night and remaining after the last.
- [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md): arrives each evening, gone each dawn.
- [The Gold](piece_the_gold.md): present each dawn for the moment before the king's men take it.

## Offers

The room offers the condition under which [Spinning](process_spinning.md) is inevitable. The task is impossible without Rumpelstilz. He arrives because the need is present and the need calls him. The room does not arrange this. The room simply holds the straw and the wheel and the locked door and allows the process to run.

The room offers three nights. Each night is a complete instance of the same transaction. Each morning the gold is taken and the room is refilled with straw. The room does not remember the previous night. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) does.

The room offers the [Debtor](position_debtor.md) the experience of being saved in a place she cannot leave. Salvation and imprisonment are the same room. The candle burns. The wheel turns. She watches. She does not spin.

## Withheld

The room does not hold the fact that the door is not locked from inside. She does not try it after the first night. The room does not offer this information. The room holds the door closed and the door remains closed and no one asks whether it had to.

The room does not hold the fact that the straw and the gold are evidence. Each morning the king takes the gold and sees proof that the miller told the truth. The miller lied. The gold is real. The room holds both of these facts simultaneously and releases neither.

The room does not hold what [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) needs. The need enters with him each night and leaves with him each morning, unchanged, un-entered in any ledger. The room has hosted three transactions and none of them addressed the thing that drove the man to the wheel.

The room does not hold the mother. She is in the mill. The daughter is in the tower. Neither can reach the other. Neither is named in this room. The room holds the daughter's survival and withholds the fact that her survival was purchased in a place her mother does not know exists, at a cost her mother will never be told.
