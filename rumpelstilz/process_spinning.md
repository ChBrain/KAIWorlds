# Process: Spinning

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Initiated by

The [Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) is locked from outside. The straw is there. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) is there. Morning will bring the king. The king has said: spin this into gold or die. She cannot spin. She has nothing left to offer except what she does not yet have. The condition that makes this process inevitable is not the straw. It is that she has already run out of things to give and the debt has not yet been named.

The [Spinner](position_spinner.md) enters. No door opens. He is simply there.

## Direction

Toward [The Gold](piece_the_gold.md). The wheel turns. [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) feeds in. The gold comes out. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) watches. She does not spin. She does not touch the wheel. The direction is one: the impossible task becomes possible, and the price for its becoming is entered into the Yearbook of [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md).

The direction is not toward her survival. Her survival is the byproduct. The direction is toward the entry in the Yearbook. This is what the [Spinner](position_spinner.md) is moving toward, and he cannot say so, and so he moves toward the gold instead.

## Lever

Carrying force: the need that cannot be named. The [Spinner](position_spinner.md) does not spin for payment. He spins because she needs him to. The need is the force. The payment is the encoding of a need so illegible to her that it must arrive as a price. He cannot say: I spin because your need is the only thing that has ever called to me. He says instead: what will you give me. The wheel turns on the gap between what he means and what he says.

Resistance: the asymmetry of the exchange. She receives survival. He receives an object, a ring, a promise. None of these is what he is spinning for. The resistance is structural: what he needs cannot be given in [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) by this person under these conditions. The resistance does not stop the spinning. It means every entry in the Yearbook of [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) is a record of a need that was never met.

## Echo

[Collecting](process_collecting.md) inherits a [Debtor](position_debtor.md) who has survived three nights at a cost she has not yet calculated, a [Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook holding three entries, and a [Spinner](position_spinner.md) who has become a [Collector](position_collector.md) without changing his need at all. What changed: the impossible has been done, three times, and the doing has produced a debt the world will refuse to honor.
