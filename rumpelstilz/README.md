# Rumpelstilz
*A KAI World built from the 1812 Grimm text.*

The wheel is already turning when you arrive.

This world runs the Rumpelstiltskin tale from its oldest recoverable layer: before the 1857 softening, before the diminutive, before he became a comic obstacle. Rumpelstilz gives in the dark. The world takes in the light. The world does not mark the loss.

Two Yearbooks run simultaneously through every transaction. His column. Her column. Same entries. Neither party can read the other's.

---

## What this world contains

**Processes (2)**
- `process_spinning.md` — the gift that cannot say what it is
- `process_collecting.md` — the legitimate claim the world refuses to honor

**Positions (6)**
- `position_spinner.md` — does the impossible work, encodes the need as price
- `position_collector.md` — arrives with what is owed, leaves with nothing
- `position_debtor.md` — survives by deferring every cost until it has a body
- `position_namer.md` — carries the word that destroys
- `position_liar.md` — starts the chain and exits before it lands
- `position_greed.md` — the position that never hits a wall

**Pieces (4)**
- `piece_the_straw.md` — the miller's lie made physical
- `piece_the_gold.md` — his nature made visible, taken at dawn
- `piece_the_wheel.md` — the instrument that belongs to no one
- `piece_the_name.md` — two Yearbook columns, one word, annihilation

**Places (2)**
- `place_tower_room.md` — withholds the exit and the mother
- `place_the_fire.md` — the only room where he is not in a position

**Personas (5)**
- `persona_rumpelstilz.md` — does not check the tree line
- `persona_millers_daughter.md` — sends the messenger to find what he is called, not who he is
- `persona_the_messenger.md` — had the name after the first repetition and stayed for the rest
- `persona_the_father.md` — tells the story one sentence after the lie
- `persona_the_king.md` — never in the room where the cost of his rooms is paid

## How to deploy

Upload all .md files in this folder to a Claude project or compatible AI platform. The world runs on `instructions.md`. No further setup required.

The world opens hot. No introduction. The wheel is already turning.

Default entry: the fire, third night.
Second door: the tower room, first night.
Third door: the road where the miller speaks.

---

## Relationship to the Grimm world

This world was written from the Grimm world. Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm are the authoring frame. The Rumpelstilz world is what they were writing when they sat at their desks. The two worlds can be run separately or together.
