# Process: Collecting

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Initiated by

The child is born. The third entry in [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook has a body now. What was promised in [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) as a future thing is a present thing, breathing, in the queen's arms. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) has been building a life on top of the deferred entry for however long it took to become a queen, a wife, a mother. The entry was always there. It has been waiting for a body.

The [Collector](position_collector.md) arrives. Not immediately. Soon enough that the queen has had time to love the child. Late enough that the love is the thing that makes the arrival unbearable.

## Direction

Toward the third entry being honored or dissolved. The debt is real. The claim is legitimate. The Yearbook holds the promise and the promise holds. The direction is one: [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) came for what was promised and the world must decide whether a promise made in a locked room to a person who had no other option is a promise that holds in a palace.

The world decides it does not hold. The riddling game is offered instead. Three days to speak his name. If she speaks it he leaves with nothing. If she cannot he takes the child. The process accepts the game because the game is the last available form of the claim.

## Lever

Carrying force: the legitimacy of the debt. The necklace was given. The ring was given. The promise was made freely, witnessed by no one, in a room where the alternative was death. The debt is real. The Yearbook holds it. The [Collector](position_collector.md) arrives with nothing but the word that was given and the word is sufficient. The wheel turned. The gold was real. The morning was survived. Three times. The third price has arrived to be paid.

Resistance: the world's refusal to receive the claim. A queen cannot give her child to a creature from a locked tower room. The court has no instrument for this debt. The king does not know it exists. The promise was made below the threshold of the world that can honor it. The resistance is not cruelty. The resistance is that the debt was contracted in a place the world does not recognize as a place where real debts are made.

## Echo

The [Collector](position_collector.md) tears himself in two. Not metaphor. The name arrives inside his certainty that it could not arrive and the certainty and the name cannot occupy the same moment and he tears himself in two. The debt is not paid. The debt is destroyed along with the Collector. The child stays. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) does not look at what is happening to him. What changed: the one person in this world who gave without taking, who did the impossible and asked only for what was encoded rather than what was needed, is gone. The world continues. The world does not mark the loss.
