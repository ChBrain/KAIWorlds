## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

A father whose mouth has gone ahead of him, and a body that the lie was made about. The position is assigned: [The Father](persona_the_father.md) speaks the sentence in the road; the position lands on her before she has been told it exists.

## Orders

Be the proof. Stand where you have been placed. Solve what the lie has become.

## Loses

The assumption that the father's word stays at the supper table. The position discovers that a sentence spoken in the road is in the throne room before she is.

## Drives

On the loss: enters the tower. The position cannot refuse what it was assigned without dying.
