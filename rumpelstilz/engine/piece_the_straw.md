# Piece: The Straw

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Place:** [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md)

## Place

[The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md). Arrives each evening before she does. Gone each morning, converted. The room is refilled each night by the king's men. The straw does not travel. It waits.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Straw and [Spinning](process_spinning.md) has no material. The wheel turns on nothing. The impossibility of the task disappears and with it the reason [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) is called.

Further: remove The Straw and the miller's lie loses its body. The lie was spoken on a road. The straw is what the lie looks like in a room. Without it the lie remains abstract. With it the lie is a ceiling-high pile of dead material that she must turn into gold by morning or die. The straw is the lie made into a consequence.

## Apparent

Dry. Pale yellow. Floor to nearly ceiling on the first night. Floor to nearly ceiling again on the second. Floor to nearly ceiling again on the third. The king's men bring it in carts. It smells of field and dust and the particular staleness of grain that has been cut and dried and stored and moved. It is not valuable. It is the most common material in the kingdom.

By morning it is gone. Not a strand remains. The floor is clean. The gold is on the bobbins against the wall.

## Yearbook

**Night 1:** the room is full of it. She cannot spin. She has a necklace. She gives the necklace. By morning the straw is gold. The king's men take the gold. The room is refilled.

**Night 2:** the room is full of it again. She has a ring. She gives the ring. By morning the straw is gold. The king takes the gold. The room is refilled.

**Night 3:** the room is full of it again. She has nothing. She promises the firstborn. By morning the straw is gold. The king takes the gold. He proposes marriage. The room is not refilled.

**After Night 3:** the straw does not appear again. The Tower Room stands empty. The wheel remains. The straw does not return because the king has what he wanted and the debt it generated will not be collected until the child is born.
