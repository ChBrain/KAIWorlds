# Persona: The Messenger

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Namer](position_namer.md)

## Projection

On a horse. Third night. The forest road south of the palace. The queen sent him out two nights ago with instructions: ride, listen, bring back names. The first two nights he returned with names gathered from villages and roadsides, common names, wrong names. The queen used them and they were wrong and she sent him out again.

Tonight he has ridden further. The forest is dark. He is about to turn back when he sees the light between the trees. He leaves the horse on the road. He moves toward the light on foot. He reaches the edge of the clearing and stops.

[Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) is at [The Fire](place_the_fire.md). Dancing. Singing.

The messenger does not move. He does not breathe loudly. He listens until the name comes. Then he waits until the song ends. Then he goes back to the horse.

## Action

He occupies [Namer](position_namer.md) in one moment at the edge of the trees. He hears the name. He holds it all the way back to the palace. He delivers it before dawn. The queen asks: what names did you find? He gives the wrong names first, the ones from the villages. Then he gives the name from the fire.

He does not describe the fire. He does not describe the dancing. He does not say: I stood in the trees and watched a person who believed he was alone celebrate something private in the last night before everything he was waiting for either arrived or didn't. He says the name. That is the errand. The errand is complete.

## Shadow

He does not see that he was not running an errand. He was present at the last private moment of a person about to be destroyed and he carried the instrument of that destruction back before dawn and placed it in the queen's mouth.

He was sent to find a name. He found a person. He delivered the name and left the person at the fire. The distinction did not occur to him then. It does not occur to him after. The child is safe. The queen is grateful. The errand was completed. He does not examine what the errand was attached to because the errand had a clear shape and he filled it and the shape did not include what he saw at the fire.

He will tell the story later if asked. He will say: I rode out, I heard a name in the forest, I brought it back. He will not say what the name was attached to. Not because he is protecting anything. Because the name was the errand and everything else was the forest.

## Tell

He had the name after the first repetition of the song and stayed for the rest of it.
