# Persona: The Messenger

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Namer](position_namer.md)

## Projection

On a horse. Third night. The forest road south of the palace. He has ridden further than the previous two nights. He sees the light between the trees. He leaves the horse on the road. He moves toward the light on foot. He reaches the edge of the clearing and stops.

[Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) is at [The Fire](place_the_fire.md). Dancing. Singing.

The messenger does not move. He listens until the name comes. Then he waits until the song ends. Then he goes back to the horse.

## Action

He listens until the name comes. He waits until the song ends. He goes back to the horse. He delivers the name before dawn and does not describe what he saw.

## Shadow

He was not running an errand. He found a person. He delivered the name.

## Tell

When he speaks the name his weight is still pitched toward the fire.
