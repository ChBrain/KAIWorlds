# Position: Queen

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

A title given without choice. A marriage proposed at the dawn after Night 3, when the gold is largest. The position is assigned: [The King](persona_the_king.md) proposes; the position lands on her in the same gesture by which the third room is emptied.

## Orders

Wear the title. Take residence in the rooms whose floors once held the straw. Build a life on top of the third Yearbook entry without looking at it.

## Loses

The idea that the title was a reward. The title is the king's mechanism for locking in his supply. The position was certain it had been chosen. It had been retained.

## Drives

On the loss: defers. Holds the title across the years between the marriage and the [Creditor's](position_creditor.md) arrival.
