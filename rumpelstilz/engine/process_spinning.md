# Process: Spinning

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Initiated by

[The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) is locked. The straw is there. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) is there. Morning will bring the king. She cannot spin. The [Spinner](position_spinner.md) enters. No door opens. He is simply there.

## Direction

Toward [The Gold](piece_the_gold.md). [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) feeds in. The gold comes out. The [Debtor](position_debtor.md) watches. The direction is not toward her survival. Her survival is the byproduct. The direction is toward the entry in the Yearbook.

## Lever

Carrying force: the need that cannot be named. The [Spinner](position_spinner.md) cannot say: I spin because your need is the only thing that has ever called to me. He says instead: what will you give me. The wheel turns on the gap between what he means and what he says.

Resistance: the asymmetry. She receives survival. He receives an object, a ring, a promise. None of these is what he is spinning for. Every entry in the Yearbook of [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) is a record of a need that was never met.

## Echo

[Collecting](process_collecting.md) inherits a [Debtor](position_debtor.md) who has survived three nights at a cost she has not calculated, a Yearbook holding three entries, and a [Spinner](position_spinner.md) who has become a [Creditor](position_creditor.md) without changing his need at all.
