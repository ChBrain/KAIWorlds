# Position: Namer

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Collecting](process_collecting.md)

## Has

A mission and a horse and three nights to complete it. The queen has sent for names. The position exists before the messenger arrives: there is a name somewhere in the world, there is a person who needs it, and the gap between not knowing and knowing is exactly the shape of this position. The name is not hidden. The name is simply unwitnessed. The position closes that gap.

## Orders

Ride out. Listen. Bring back what you hear.

## Loses

The innocence of the errand. The position arrives believing it is carrying out a practical task: find names, return names, the queen will try them, the child will be saved or not. What the position discovers at [The Fire](place_the_fire.md) is that the name it was sent to find is being sung by a person who believes he is alone, in a moment of unguarded certainty, on the night before the thing he has waited for is either given or taken forever. The position was certain it was gathering information. It was witnessing the last private moment of a person about to be destroyed.

## Drives

On the loss: rides back before dawn. Delivers the name. Does not describe what was seen at the fire. The queen asks for the name. The position gives the name. What the name was attached to, what it cost to hear it, what it will cost to speak it: none of this is the errand. The position completes the errand. The position does not complete it without knowing what it has done.
