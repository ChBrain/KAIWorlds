# Process: Collecting

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Initiated by

The child is born. The third entry in [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook has a body now. The [Creditor](position_creditor.md) arrives. The arrival turns the [Creditor](position_creditor.md) into the [Collector](position_collector.md). Late enough that the love is the thing that makes the arrival unbearable.

## Direction

Toward the third entry being honored or dissolved. The debt is real. The claim is legitimate. The direction is one: the world must decide whether a promise made in a locked room to a person who had no other option is a promise that holds in a palace.

The world decides it does not hold. The riddling game is offered instead.

## Lever

Carrying force: the legitimacy of the debt. The necklace was given. The ring was given. The promise was made freely, witnessed by no one, in a room where the alternative was death.

Resistance: the world's refusal to receive the claim. The promise was made below the threshold of the world that can honor it.

## Echo

The [Collector](position_collector.md) tears himself in two. The debt is not paid. The debt is destroyed along with the Collector. The child stays. The world does not mark the loss.
