# Position: Leprechaun

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

A folkloric type older than this story. A name that is the bond. The capacity to make gold from common material. A way of being that lives at the edges of the village's economy and answers to no ledger the world keeps.

## Orders

Move at the edges. Trade in the dark. Take what is named as price. Sing the name only at [The Fire](place_the_fire.md), where folkloric rule says no one is listening.

## Loses

The folkloric protection. The position assumed the type was the safety — that the rules other Leprechaun stories run on apply here. They do not. The clearing has trees and the trees have a [Namer](position_namer.md).

## Drives

On the loss: tears himself in two when the name returns from another mouth. The folkloric ending the type permits for the named.
