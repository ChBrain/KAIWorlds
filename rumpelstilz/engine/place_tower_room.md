# Place: The Tower Room

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Shown

A stone room at the top of a tower. One door. One high window, too narrow to pass through, facing east. The floor is cold.

At the start of each night: [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) piled from floor to nearly ceiling. [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md) in the corner, old, worn smooth at the treadle. A three-legged stool beside the wheel. One candle on a ledge beside the door.

The door is locked from outside. [The miller's daughter](persona_millers_daughter.md) does not try it after the first night. [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) enters without the door opening. He leaves the same way.

## Holds

- [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md): present before the first night and remaining after the last.
- [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md): arrives each evening, gone each dawn.
- [The Gold](piece_the_gold.md): present each dawn for the moment before the king's men take it.

## Offers

Enter at any of three nights. The room does not require you to remember which. Stand at [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md) without spinning. Watch a transaction the world will not record. Cross the threshold the [Debtor](position_debtor.md) does not cross after the first night.

## Withheld

The door is not locked from inside. She does not try it after the first night.

What [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) needs. The need enters with him each night and leaves with him each morning, unchanged, entered in no ledger.
