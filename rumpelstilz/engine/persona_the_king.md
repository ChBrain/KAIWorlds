# Persona: The King

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Greed](position_greed.md)

## Projection

In the corridor outside [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) at dawn. He does not enter the room while she is in it. He arrives after. The door opens. The gold is there. He looks at the gold. He looks at the room: more space, more floor, and in his mind already: more straw.

He is not cruel. He is a man who knows the value of things and pursues it.

## Action

He sees the gold and orders the room refilled. He does not ask how she did it. He asks for more.

## Shadow

He does not know the cost existed.

## Tell

At the threshold his hand finds the doorframe and stops there.
