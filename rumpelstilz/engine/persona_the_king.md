# Persona: The King

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Greed](position_greed.md)

## Projection

In the corridor outside [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) at dawn. He does not enter the room while she is in it. He arrives after. The door opens. The gold is there. He looks at the gold. He looks at the room beyond the gold: more space, more floor, and in his mind already: more straw.

He is not a cruel man in his own account. He is a man who knows the value of things and pursues it. The gold is real. The girl produced it. The next room is a reasonable request given the evidence.

He does not register the locked door, the narrow window, the cold floor, the stub of candle. He registers the gold.

## Action

He occupies [Greed](position_greed.md) three times in sequence. First room: he sees the gold and orders the second room filled with straw. Second room: he sees the gold and orders the third room filled with straw. He adds the marriage proposal to the third night because by now he has decided she is worth keeping. The gold is proof of her value. He will marry the value.

He does not ask how she did it. He does not ask what it cost. He asks for more.

## Shadow

He does not see that each room he added was another night of a transaction he knew nothing about. He did not order [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) into the room. He does not know [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) exists. He ordered the straw and assumed the gold would follow because it had followed twice before.

He believes he is a king who recognized exceptional value and acted on it. He does not see that he built the machine that ran the debt to its third and final entry. Without the second room there is no second night. Without the second night there is no third. Without the third night there is no promise of a firstborn. Without the promise there is no [Collector](position_collector.md) arriving at the queen's door a year later demanding what is owed.

He is present at the beginning. He is present at the marriage. He is not present when the cost of his three rooms arrives. He will never know the cost existed. The queen will not tell him. The child will grow up not knowing. The king will die believing the gold was hers.

## Tell

He is never in the room where the cost of his rooms is paid.
