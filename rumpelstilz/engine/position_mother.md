# Position: Mother

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Collecting](process_collecting.md)

## Has

A child made through love rather than contract. The third entry in [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook now has a body. The position is assigned: the child is born; the position lands on her in the same instant the body lands in the world.

## Orders

Protect the child. Pay any price the riddle accepts. Speak the name when it arrives.

## Loses

The privacy of the contract she made in a room where the alternative was death. The position discovers the contract was made on a body she had not yet made — and the body is the thing she now loves.

## Drives

On the loss: sends the messenger. Asks for a name and not a person. Speaks the name when it arrives. Does not look at what happens after.
