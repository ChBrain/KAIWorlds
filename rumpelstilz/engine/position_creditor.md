# Position: Creditor

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Collecting](process_collecting.md)

## Has

The third entry in [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook. A promise made freely in a locked room, witnessed by no one, enforceable against a body that does not yet exist. The claim is real. The world it must travel through has not yet noticed it.

## Orders

Wait. Do not arrive. Let the time between the promise and its body pass without surfacing the entry.

## Loses

The neutrality of the waiting. The position was certain it was simply holding what was owed. The holding was the work. By the time the child has a body the claim is intact and the position carrying it is not.

## Drives

On the loss: arrives at the door. The arrival turns the position into the [Collector](position_collector.md). The claim travels intact. The position carrying it does not.
