# Place: The Fire

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Shown

A clearing in the forest. Night. Third night after the spinning is done and before the queen must answer.

A fire built low and careful, the way a person builds a fire when they expect to be there a long time. The wood is dry. The smoke goes straight up. The trees around the clearing are close enough to hold the warmth and far enough that the light does not reach their trunks.

[Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) is alone. He believes he is alone. He dances around the fire. He is not a graceful dancer. The name of the dance is not a name anyone else would give it. He sings.

The [Namer](position_namer.md) is at the edge of the trees. Still. The Namer does not breathe loudly. The Namer arrived before the fire was built and has not moved.

## Holds

- [The Name](piece_the_name.md): present here in its only unguarded form. Sung into the air above the fire. Available for the first and last time to anyone who is listening.

## Offers

The fire offers [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) the only place in this world where he is not in a position. Not the [Spinner](position_spinner.md). Not the [Collector](position_collector.md). Not performing a need as a price. Here he is only himself, which is a person who believes he is about to receive the one thing he has ever asked for, and who is celebrating alone because there is no one to celebrate with.

The fire offers the [Namer](position_namer.md) the word they were sent to find. The Namer does not have to search. The Namer only has to stay still and listen. The word arrives on its own. This is the only place in the world where the name is spoken freely, without duress, without encoding, without the gap between what he means and what he says.

The fire offers the world a single moment of Rumpelstilz being exactly what he is. The moment is witnessed. The witnessing ends the moment.

## Withheld

The fire does not hold the fact that the [Namer](position_namer.md) is there. Rumpelstilz does not look toward the trees. The fire is bright and the trees are dark and certainty does the rest. He has built fires in other clearings in other forests and sung his name into the air above them and nothing has ever moved in the trees. The fire does not correct this expectation. The fire burns and the Namer listens and the certainty holds until morning.

The fire does not hold what the dancing costs him. He is not a graceful dancer. The dance is not for an audience. It is the physical form of a relief so large it has to go somewhere. The relief is that after years of encoding a need as a price, the price is about to be paid, and the need is about to be met. He does not know the need will not be met. The fire does not tell him.

The fire does not hold the fact that this is the last night the name belongs only to him. By dawn it will be in the mouth of the messenger. By the third day it will have been spoken by the queen as a weapon. The fire holds the last moment of the name's safety and releases nothing about what comes after.
