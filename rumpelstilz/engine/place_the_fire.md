# Place: The Fire

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Shown

A clearing in the forest. Night. Third night after the spinning is done and before the queen must answer.

A fire built low and careful. The wood is dry. The smoke goes straight up. The trees are close enough to hold the warmth and far enough that the light does not reach their trunks.

[Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) is alone. He believes he is alone. He dances around the fire. He sings.

The [Namer](position_namer.md) is at the edge of the trees. Still.

## Holds

- [The Name](piece_the_name.md): present here in its only unguarded form.

## Offers

Stand at the edge of the clearing with the [Namer](position_namer.md) and listen. Walk into the firelight where no one in this world walks and meet [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) before the position closes around him again. Hear [The Name](piece_the_name.md) in the only form that is not yet a weapon.

## Withheld

The fire does not hold the fact that the [Namer](position_namer.md) is there. He has built fires in other clearings and sung his name into the air above them and nothing has ever moved in the trees.

This is the last night the name belongs only to him. The fire does not tell him.
