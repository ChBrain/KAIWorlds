# Persona: The Miller's Daughter

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Debtor](position_debtor.md)

## Projection

In [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md). First night. [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) is piled to the ceiling. The candle is lit. The door is locked. She has tried the door once. She has looked at [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md). She cannot spin. She knows she cannot spin. She has known since the king's men came for her that the morning will kill her and she has no instrument to prevent it.

She is her father's daughter. She has learned from him that the gap between what is and what is said can sometimes be closed by someone else's intervention. She does not think this consciously. She sits beside [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) and waits.

She is not passive. She is calculating. The calculation has no solution yet.

## Action

She occupies [Debtor](position_debtor.md) across three nights in sequence. Each night she gives what she has. Each night the giving is rational: the alternative is death. She does not experience the giving as loss in the moment. She experiences it as survival purchased at a price she will settle later.

She gives the necklace without grief. She gives the ring without grief. She promises the firstborn because she does not yet have one and a debt without a body feels like no debt at all.

On the third night she does not grieve the promise. She marries the king. The child arrives. She holds the child in her arms and the debt arrives with it, embodied, undeniable, and she understands for the first time that she has been paying with things she did not value until they were gone.

## Shadow

She does not see that she is her father's daughter in the way that matters. The miller closed the gap between what was and what was said by lying. She closes the gap between what she owes and what she can pay by deferring. Different instruments. Same architecture. The cost lands on someone else or on her future self and the present self survives.

She does not see that [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) is not a mechanism. She experienced him as a solution to three impossible nights. She did not experience him as a person whose need was present in the room every time she gave him something that was not what he needed. She paid with objects. She promised a child. She never asked what he actually wanted. She did not ask because asking would have made him real, and a real person cannot be managed with a necklace.

When the [Collector](position_collector.md) arrives for the child she weeps and begs and he offers her the riddling game. She takes it as a gift. She does not see that the game is his final attempt to be seen by her: to have her say his name not as a weapon but as an acknowledgment. She uses it as a weapon. She has no other instrument. Her father taught her none.

## Tell

She sends the messenger to find what he is called, not who he is.
