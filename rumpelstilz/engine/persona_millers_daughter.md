## Projection

In [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md). First night. [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) is piled to the ceiling. The candle is lit. The door is locked. She has tried the door once. She has looked at [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md). She cannot spin.

She is not passive. She is calculating. The calculation has no solution yet.

## Action

She gives what she has. The giving is rational: the alternative is death. She does not experience it as loss. She experiences it as survival purchased at a price she will settle later.

## Shadow

She did not ask because asking would have made him real.

## Tell

She sends the messenger to find what he is called, not who he is.
