# Position: Collector

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Collecting](process_collecting.md)

## Has

A legitimate claim. The [Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook holds three entries. The third entry is a firstborn child, promised freely, witnessed by no one, enforceable by nothing except the word that was given.

## Orders

Arrive. Present the claim. Take what is owed.

## Loses

The legitimacy of the claim in the eyes of the world. The debt is real. The world will not receive it.

## Drives

On the loss: accepts the riddling game. Three days to speak the name. If she cannot name him he takes the child. If she can he leaves with nothing. Either way he will have been seen by her as something more than a mechanism.
