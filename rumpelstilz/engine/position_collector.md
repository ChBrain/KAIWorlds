# Position: Collector

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Collecting](process_collecting.md)

## Has

A legitimate claim. The [Straw→Gold](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook holds three entries. The third entry is a firstborn child, promised freely, witnessed by no one, enforceable by nothing except the word that was given. The position exists before [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) arrives at the queen's door: there is a debt, there is a debtor, and the debt is due.

## Orders

Arrive. Present the claim. Take what is owed.

## Loses

The legitimacy of the claim in the eyes of the world. The position arrives certain that a debt is a debt: that what was promised in the tower is owed in the palace, that the change in her circumstances does not alter the terms, that a queen is still the miller's daughter who made the promise. What the position discovers is that the world does not honor debts it finds inconvenient. The claim is real. The world will not receive it. The position was certain it was owed. It is owed. The owing is insufficient.

## Drives

On the loss: accepts the riddling game. Three days to speak the name. The position did not negotiate this. The position accepts because the acceptance is the last available form of the claim: if she cannot name him he takes the child, and if she can he leaves with nothing, and either way he will have been seen by her as something more than a mechanism. The game is not a concession. The game is the need encoding itself one final time as a price.
