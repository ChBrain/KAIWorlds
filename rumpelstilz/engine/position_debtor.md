# Position: Debtor

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Spinning](process_spinning.md)

## Has

An impossible task and no means to complete it. A locked door. A morning that will kill her if the straw is not gold by dawn. The [Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook is empty. It will not remain empty.

## Orders

Survive the night. Pay whatever is asked. Do not examine the cost until the morning is safe.

## Loses

The future. The position was certain it was managing a series of transactions. It was compounding a debt against a life it had not yet lived.

## Drives

On the loss: defers. The child is not yet born. The position marries the king. It builds a life on top of the deferred entry in the Yearbook and does not look at it until the [Collector](position_collector.md) arrives at the door.
