# Position: Debtor

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Spinning](process_spinning.md)

## Has

An impossible task and no means to complete it. A locked door. A morning that will kill her if the straw is not gold by dawn. The position exists before the [miller's daughter](persona_millers_daughter.md) arrives in [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md): there is a demand, there is a person who cannot meet it, and the gap between what is required and what is possible is exactly the shape of this position. The [Straw](piece_the_straw.md) Yearbook is empty. It will not remain empty.

## Orders

Survive the night. Pay whatever is asked. Do not examine the cost until the morning is safe.

## Loses

The future. The position arrives believing the payment is bounded: a necklace is a necklace, a ring is a ring, finite objects given for a finite service. What the position discovers across three nights is that the Yearbook does not close. The first payment purchases one morning. The second purchases another. The third purchases a morning that does not yet exist with a child that does not yet exist. The position was certain it was managing a series of transactions. It was compounding a debt against a life it had not yet lived.

## Drives

On the loss: defers. The child is not yet born. The debt is real but its body has not arrived. The position does not examine what was promised. The position marries the king. The position builds a life on top of the deferred entry in the Yearbook and does not look at it until the [Collector](position_collector.md) arrives at the door with the child in the room behind her.
