# Piece: The Name

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Persona:** [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md)

## Place

[The Fire](place_the_fire.md). Until the night the [Namer](position_namer.md) hears it. After that it lives in the mouth of the queen, once, and then nowhere.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Name and [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) cannot be destroyed. The debt runs forever. The child is taken.

Remove The Name and the two Yearbook columns never converge. The name is the single point where her story and his story occupy the same word at the same moment.

## Apparent

A word in German. Compound. Two roots pressed together into one unlovely sound. It names something that makes noise and moves badly.

He sings it at [The Fire](place_the_fire.md) on the third night, alone, certain. The singing is not performance. It is the sound a person makes when they believe no one is listening: unguarded, complete, slightly off-key.

The [Namer](position_namer.md) is in the trees.

## Yearbook

His column: the name has always been his. He has sung it at fires in other forests. He has never been heard. The name is the one thing in his life that belongs entirely to him.

Night 3 at the fire: he sings it into the air above the flames. This is the last night the name belongs only to him.

Day 3 in the throne room: the queen speaks it back to him. The two columns converge at this word and the convergence is annihilation.

Her column: the name arrives before dawn. She hears it once. She holds it all day. She says wrong names first. She watches his face. Then she says it. She does not look at what happens after. She never speaks the name again. It was a key and the door is gone.
