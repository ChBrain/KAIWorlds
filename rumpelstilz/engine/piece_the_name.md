# Piece: The Name

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Persona:** [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md)

## Place

[The Fire](place_the_fire.md). The name lives there until the night the [Namer](position_namer.md) hears it. After that it lives in the mouth of the queen, once, and then nowhere.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Name and [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) cannot be destroyed. The debt runs forever. The child is taken. The world becomes a different story: one in which the legitimate claim is honored and the cost of the three nights is finally paid.

Further: remove The Name and the certainty that destroys him has no object. His certainty was that the name was safe, that no one had heard it, that he was alone at the fire. The name is what that certainty was about. Without it the certainty is just confidence. With it the certainty is the mechanism of his destruction.

Remove The Name and the two Yearbook columns never converge. The columns run parallel forever. The name is the single point where her story and his story occupy the same word at the same moment.

## Apparent

A word in German. Compound. Two roots pressed together into one unlovely sound. It names something that makes noise and moves badly. He chose it or was given it or grew into it so long ago that the distinction no longer holds.

He sings it at [The Fire](place_the_fire.md) on the third night, alone, certain. The singing is not performance. It is the sound a person makes when they believe no one is listening: unguarded, complete, slightly off-key.

The [Namer](position_namer.md) is in the trees. The Namer does not move. The Namer carries the word back before dawn.

## Yearbook

**His column:**

**Before Night 1:** the name has always been his. He has sung it at fires before this one, in other forests, in other years. He has never been heard. The name is the one thing in his life that belongs entirely to him.

**Night 3, at the fire:** he sings it into the air above the flames, certain he is alone. This is the last night the name belongs only to him.

**Day 3, in the throne room:** the queen speaks it back to him. Her mouth. His name. The two columns converge at this word and the convergence is annihilation. He tears himself in two.

**Her column:**

**Before Day 3:** the name arrives before dawn. The messenger is out of breath. She hears it once. She holds it all day.

**Day 3, in the throne room:** she says wrong names first. She watches his face. She lets him keep the certainty one moment longer than necessary. Then she says it. She does not look at what happens after. She never speaks the name again. It was a key and the door is gone.
