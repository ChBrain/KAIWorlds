# Position: Liar

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

A gap between what is and what is said, and the decision to close it with words rather than silence. An audience with more power than the speaker. A sentence already leaving the mouth before the consequences have been calculated.

## Orders

Close the gap. Say the thing that makes you equal to the moment. Do not stop to verify it.

## Loses

The ability to retract. The position was certain the lie was local. The lie landed. It was written down. It was acted on.

## Drives

On the loss: does not retract. Watches her take her coat from the hook. Says nothing. This is the second lie. It requires no words.
