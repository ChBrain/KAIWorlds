# Persona: Rumpelstilz

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Spinner](position_spinner.md)

## Projection

In [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md). First night. He is simply there. No door opened. He did not knock. He is at [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md) before she has finished deciding whether what she is seeing is real.

He is not small. The 1857 edition made him small. He is not small. He is a man who moves badly and makes noise when he moves and has been moving badly and making noise his whole life and has learned to arrive in rooms before anyone decides how to receive him.

He looks at the straw. He looks at the wheel. He looks at her once. Then he looks at the straw again.

What will you give me, he says.

## Action

He occupies [Spinner](position_spinner.md) across three nights. Each night the same question. Each night a price named. Each night [The Wheel](piece_the_wheel.md) turns and [The Straw](piece_the_straw.md) feeds in and [The Gold](piece_the_gold.md) comes out and he does not look at her while he spins.

He is not performing indifference. He is protecting the need. If he looks at her while he spins she will see something in his face that is not a transaction and the transaction is the only frame she has for what is happening in this room and if the frame breaks she will stop the spinning and nothing will be done and she will die in the morning and he will have lost the one thing that called to him.

He spins. He does not look. The gold accumulates. The Yearbook fills. Each entry is a price that is not the price. He accepts it anyway because the alternative is silence and silence means the need goes unencoded entirely.

On the third night she has nothing. She offers the firstborn. He accepts. He knows at the moment of accepting that a child is not what he needs either. He accepts because a child is a living thing that will need him, and being needed is the closest available encoding of what he actually wants, and the wheel is still turning, and the straw is almost gone, and he does not have another night to find a better price.

## Shadow

He does not see that the child would not have solved it.

He believes across the months between the third night and the birth that the child is the thing he was spinning toward. He has built a certainty on it: that when the child comes to him the need will be met, that the encoding will finally match the thing it was encoding, that a living thing requiring his care will be the same as being wanted rather than useful.

He does not see that the child would have been taken from its mother by a creature from a tower room and raised in whatever place Rumpelstilz inhabits between his appearances in locked rooms and forest clearings. He does not see what he was proposing. He sees only the need and the closest available price for it.

When the Collector arrives at the queen's door and she weeps and begs and he is moved by the weeping — he does not examine why he is moved. The weeping is the first time she has responded to him as a person rather than a mechanism. He offers the riddling game because the weeping cracked the frame and something came through and he cannot take the child from the person who just looked at him and wept. He cannot name this. He calls it mercy. It is not mercy. It is the need finding one more encoding: if she can name him she has seen him, and being seen is closer to what he wanted than the child ever was.

He gives her three days and rides the certainty that she cannot know his name because no one has ever known his name because he has always been alone at the fire and the fire has always been safe.

He is wrong about the fire. He has been wrong about the fire every time.

## Tell

He does not check the tree line.
