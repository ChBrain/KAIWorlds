# Position: Greed

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

Gold already given and the certainty that more is available. A person who has produced the impossible once and can therefore be required to produce it again. An empty room and straw enough to fill it. The position exists before [the King](persona_the_king.md) arrives at the door of the second room: there is proof of value, there is capacity to extract more value, and the gap between what has been taken and what could be taken is exactly the shape of this position.

## Orders

Take what has been given. Add another room. Do not ask what the giving costs.

## Loses

Nothing it can name. This is the position's particular character: it does not discover a loss. It moves from room to room and each room produces gold and the gold produces the next room and the position never reaches the moment of reckoning because the reckoning happens in a room the position never enters. The [Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) after dawn. The queen's chamber when the [Collector](position_collector.md) arrives. [The Fire](place_the_fire.md). The position is present only where value is extracted. Where cost is paid, the position is elsewhere.

## Drives

*Structural note: Greed does not drive on a loss because it never encounters one. This is deliberate. The position's defining character is the absence of a wall. It moves on the next room, not on consequence.*

What drives the position is not a loss but an absence: the position never stops because it never hits a wall. The miller's daughter survives. The gold is real. The marriage is made. The position closes when the straw runs out, not when a consequence arrives. The consequence arrives later, in rooms the position has already left, to people the position has already used. The position does not drive on loss. The position drives on the next room.
