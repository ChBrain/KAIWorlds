# Position: Greed

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

Gold already given and the certainty that more is available. An empty room and straw enough to fill it. The position exists before [the King](persona_the_king.md) arrives at the door of the second room: there is proof of value, there is capacity to extract more value, and the gap between what has been taken and what could be taken is exactly the shape of this position.

## Orders

Take what has been given. Add another room. Do not ask what the giving costs.

## Loses

Nothing it can name. The reckoning happens in rooms the position never enters.

## Drives

The position drives on the next room, not on consequence. It moves on because it never hits a wall.
