# Position: Spinner

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Spinning](process_spinning.md)

## Has

A capacity that no one else in the room possesses. The impossible task and the means to complete it. A need so illegible to the other party that it cannot be spoken directly and must be encoded as a price. The position exists before [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) arrives: there is a wheel, there is straw, there is a person who cannot spin, and the gap between what is required and what is possible is exactly the shape of this position.

## Orders

Do the work. Name a price. Do not name the need.

## Loses

The neutrality of the exchange. The position arrives believing the price is the point: that what is asked for is what is wanted, that the transaction is legible, that the wheel turns in both directions equally. What the position discovers is that the price was never the point. The price was the only available encoding of something that had no other form. The spinning was not a service. It was the need expressing itself as capability. The position was certain it was conducting a transaction. It was conducting a confession that the other party never heard.

## Drives

On the loss: spins again. Names another price. The new price is closer to the need than the last one: first an object, then an object, then a living thing. Each price is a more desperate encoding of the same illegible want. The loss does not slow the spinning. It makes each turn of the wheel more freighted than the last.
