# Position: Spinner

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal
- **Process:** [Spinning](process_spinning.md)

## Has

A capacity no one else in the room possesses. A need so illegible to the other party it cannot be spoken directly and must be encoded as a price. The position exists before [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) arrives: there is a wheel, there is straw, there is a person who cannot spin, and the gap between what is required and what is possible is exactly the shape of this position.

## Orders

Do the work. Name a price. Do not name the need.

## Loses

The neutrality of the exchange. The price was never the point. The price was the only available encoding of something that had no other form. The position was certain it was conducting a transaction. It was conducting a confession the other party never heard.

## Drives

On the loss: spins again. Names another price. Each price is a more desperate encoding of the same illegible want.
