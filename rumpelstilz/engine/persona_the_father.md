# Persona: The Father

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz

## Title
[Liar](position_liar.md)

## Projection

A miller. Standing at the edge of a conversation that has gone further than he intended. The king's man is still listening. The miller's mouth is still moving.

He says she can spin straw into gold.

He is standing in the road. The king's man has a horse. The miller has flour on his coat.

## Action

He speaks the sentence. He watches the king's man write it down. He goes home. He does not tell his daughter what he said.

## Shadow

The sentence left his mouth and kept moving without him.

## Tell

When he tells the story later he begins one sentence after the lie.
