# Piece: The Gold

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Persona:** [The King](persona_the_king.md)

## Place

With [The King](persona_the_king.md). It leaves [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) each morning with his men and does not return.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Gold and [Greed](position_greed.md) has no object. The king refills the room because the gold was there the morning before. Without the gold there is no second room, no third room, no marriage proposal. The entire chain from tower to palace to throne collapses.

Further: remove The Gold and [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md)'s nature has no evidence. The gold is the only visible proof that something impossible happened in the locked room. Without it the three nights are unwitnessed. With it they are documented each morning in the king's treasury. The gold is the record of the spinning that no one but the [Debtor](position_debtor.md) saw.

## Apparent

Gold thread on bobbins, stacked against the wall of [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) at dawn. Catching the first light through the high window. Still warm from the wheel. The king's men load it into chests. The chests go to the treasury. It is not examined closely. It is counted.

It looks like any gold. There is nothing in it that records where it came from. No one who handles it afterward knows it was straw the night before.

## Yearbook

**Dawn, Night 1:** three bobbins. Enough to confirm the miller's claim. The king orders the second room filled. The gold goes to the treasury. It is entered in no ledger that names its source.

**Dawn, Night 2:** more bobbins. Enough to change the king's calculation from confirmation to acquisition. He begins to think about marriage. The gold goes to the treasury.

**Dawn, Night 3:** the largest quantity. The king proposes. The gold goes to the treasury. It sits there among the kingdom's other gold, indistinguishable, unaware that it was purchased with a necklace, a ring, and a child not yet born.

**After the birth:** it remains in the treasury. After [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) tears himself in two the gold does not change. It bears no mark of what produced it. It is the only thing in this world that carries no weight of what it cost.
