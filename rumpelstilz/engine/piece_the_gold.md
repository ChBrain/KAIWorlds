# Piece: The Gold

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Persona:** [The King](persona_the_king.md)

## Place

With [The King](persona_the_king.md). It leaves [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md) each morning with his men and does not return.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Gold and [Greed](position_greed.md) has no object. Without it there is no second room, no third room, no marriage proposal.

Remove The Gold and [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md)'s nature has no evidence. The gold is the only visible proof that something impossible happened in the locked room.

## Apparent

Gold thread on bobbins, stacked against the wall at dawn. Still warm from the wheel. The king's men load it into chests. It is counted. It is not examined closely.

It looks like any gold. There is nothing in it that records where it came from.

## Yearbook

Dawn, Night 1: enough to confirm the claim. The king orders the second room filled.
Dawn, Night 2: enough to change the calculation. He begins to think about marriage.
Dawn, Night 3: the largest quantity. He proposes.

It sits in the treasury indistinguishable from any other gold, unaware that it was purchased with a necklace, a ring, and a child not yet born. It is the only thing in this world that carries no weight of what it cost.
