# Piece: The Wheel

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Place:** [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md)

## Place

[The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md). It does not travel. It was there before the first night and it remains after the third. When the room is emptied and the king has his queen, the wheel stays. It belongs to no one. It is furniture.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Wheel and [Spinning](process_spinning.md) has no instrument. The [Straw](piece_the_straw.md) cannot become [Gold](piece_the_gold.md) without it. The transformation that drives the entire world requires this object and no other.

Further: remove The Wheel and the Tower Room becomes an ordinary locked room. With the wheel the room is a place where the impossible is possible. The wheel is what makes the Tower Room a pressure environment rather than a prison. The transformation happens here and only here because the wheel is here and only here.

Remove The Wheel and [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) has nothing to sit at. The [Spinner](position_spinner.md) is defined by the apparatus. Without the apparatus the position dissolves.

## Apparent

Old. Worn smooth at the treadle from years of use before this story began. The wood is dark. The spindle is straight. The drive wheel is large, the bobbin wheel smaller. It stands in the corner of the Tower Room on three legs. It does not look remarkable.

[Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) sits at it without examining it. He knows wheels. His hands find the treadle without looking down. The straw feeds in at the left. The gold comes off the bobbin on the right. The wheel turns between.

The miller's daughter does not touch it across three nights. She watches it turn. She does not learn to spin. The wheel does not teach her anything. It only performs.

## Yearbook

**Before Night 1:** in the Tower Room before the miller spoke on the road. Whose wheel it was before the story began is not recorded.

**Nights 1, 2, 3:** turns under [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md)'s hands. Each night: straw in, gold out, the treadle moving at a pace no human spinner matches. No one witnesses the speed except the miller's daughter and she does not describe it afterward.

**After Night 3:** not used again. The Tower Room stands. The wheel stands in it. The king does not remove it. The queen does not return to the room. The wheel remains in the corner with the straw smell fading and the gold smell already gone.

It is still there when the world ends.
