# Piece: The Wheel

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Place:** [The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md)

## Place

[The Tower Room](place_tower_room.md). It does not travel. It was there before the first night and remains after the third.

## Load Bearing

Remove The Wheel and [Spinning](process_spinning.md) has no instrument. The transformation requires this object and no other.

Remove The Wheel and [Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) has nothing to sit at. The [Spinner](position_spinner.md) is defined by the apparatus.

## Apparent

Old. Worn smooth at the treadle. The wood is dark. The spindle is straight. It stands in the corner on three legs. It does not look remarkable.

[Rumpelstilz](persona_rumpelstilz.md) sits at it without examining it. His hands find the treadle without looking down. The straw feeds in at the left. The gold comes off the bobbin on the right.

The miller's daughter does not touch it across three nights.

## Yearbook

Night 1: straw in, gold out.
Night 2: straw in, gold out.
Night 3: straw in, gold out. The room is not refilled.

After Night 3: not used again. The king does not remove it. The queen does not return. It is still there when the world ends.
