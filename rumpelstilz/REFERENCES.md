# References

## Primary sources

Brothers Grimm, *Kinder- und Hausmärchen*, Volume 1, first edition. Berlin: Realschulbuchhandlung, 1812. KHM 55: Rumpelstilzchen.

Brothers Grimm, *Kinder- und Hausmärchen*, seventh edition. Göttingen: Dieterich, 1857. Referenced as comparative text and evidence of editorial smoothing. Not the operating version of this world.

## Secondary sources

Rölleke, Heinz, ed. *Die älteste Märchensammlung der Brüder Grimm*. Cologne-Geneva: Fondation Martin Bodmer, 1975. Critical edition of the Ölenberg manuscript, the earliest surviving draft.

Zipes, Jack. *The Brothers Grimm: From Enchanted Forests to the Modern World*. New York: Palgrave Macmillan, 2002.

da Silva, Sara Graça and Tehrani, Jamshid J. "Comparative phylogenetic analyses uncover the ancient roots of Indo-European folktales." *Royal Society Open Science*, 2016. On the 4,000-year ancestry of ATU 500 tale types.
