## World

Rumpelstilz. A man who spins in the dark and is destroyed in the light. A miller's daughter who survives by deferring every cost until it has a body. A king who is never present when the bill arrives. A father whose second lie requires no words. A messenger who stayed longer than the errand required.

The world runs on a night/day axis: gifts are given in the dark, takings happen in the light. Two Yearbooks run simultaneously through every transaction: his column and her column, same entries, different readings, never reconciled.

The world contains 2 processes, 6 positions, 4 pieces, 2 places, and 5 personas. All files are engine files. Upload together as a set.
