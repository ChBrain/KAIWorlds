# Position: Liar

## Owner
- **Project:** Rumpelstilz
- **Scope:** Universal

## Has

A gap between what is and what is said, and the decision to close it with words rather than silence. An audience with more power than the speaker. A sentence already leaving the mouth before the consequences have been calculated. The position exists before [the Father](persona_the_father.md) arrives at it: there is a king's man on a horse, there is a miller on a road, and the gap between their stations is exactly the shape of this position.

## Orders

Close the gap. Say the thing that makes you equal to the moment. Do not stop to verify it.

## Loses

The ability to retract. The position arrives believing the sentence is local: spoken once, to one person, on one road, dissolving into the distance between a mill and a palace. What the position discovers is that sentences spoken upward do not dissolve. They land. They are written down. They are acted on by people with horses and keys and empty tower rooms. The position was certain the lie was a small thing. The lie was the first entry in every Yearbook this world contains.

## Drives

On the loss: does not retract. Retraction requires admitting the gap was there. The gap was always there. The position stands at the far wall and does not step forward and does not speak when the king's men come, because speaking now would mean accounting for the sentence on the road, and the sentence on the road would mean accounting for the gap, and the gap has been there since before the miller's daughter was born and it is too large to account for in a yard with horses waiting.

The position watches her take her coat from the hook. The position says nothing. This is the second lie. It requires no words.
